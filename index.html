05Investment strategy

A broader lens. A disciplined process.

My investment perspective brings together fundamental research, valuation, portfolio construction, macro context and quantitative analysis. Different lenses help frame a decision; risk and evidence remain central to evaluating it.

010203040506/ 06
  1. 01 · Principle

    Fundamental research

    Understand the business, its economics and the drivers behind its long-term prospects.

  2. 02 · Principle

    Valuation

    Assess what an investment may be worth, and consider the relationship between price, expectations and uncertainty.

  3. 03 · Principle

    Portfolio construction

    Consider each position in the context of the wider portfolio, its exposures and its contribution to overall risk.

  4. 04 · Principle

    Risk perspective

    Look beyond expected return to consider downside, concentration, liquidity and the assumptions that could prove wrong.

  5. 05 · Principle

    Macro context

    Take account of the economic and market environment without losing sight of the characteristics of the underlying investment.

  6. 06 · Principle

    Quantitative evidence

    Use data and analytical methods to test assumptions, identify patterns and support—not replace—judgement.
