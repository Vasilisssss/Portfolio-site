  1. 01

    Time is my edge

    [Draft] I only invest money I won’t need for years, so short-term prices don’t force my hand.

  2. 02

    Avoid the big loss

    [Draft] I size positions so that no single mistake can set me back more than I can recover from.

  3. 03

    Own what I understand

    [Draft] If I can’t explain why I own something in two sentences, I don’t own it.

  4. 04

    Keep dry powder

    [Draft] Cash is an option on future opportunities, not a wasted asset.

  5. 05

    Automate the boring part

    [Draft] Regular contributions happen on schedule; my judgement goes into the rest.

  6. 06

    Write it down
